This morning's rounds

The hedgerows moved overnight. Fix the lanes and get the post out.

Every route starts scrambled. Sort the satchel, then rotate and slide garden tiles until Pip can walk from door to door. Each move costs a cup of tea, and every route has a par found by a solver.

How to play

  • Tap a tile to turn it a quarter turn clockwise. The Turn back button turns it the other way.
  • Swipe a row or column, or tap the red arrows at the edges, to slide it one space. The end tile wraps around.
  • Houses, the post office, trees, water and footbridges never slide. Moving tiles step around them.
  • Pip sets off the moment the next stop connects, so plan the satchel order before your first move.
  • Each move pours a cup of tea. Par earns three stars, a couple over earns two, and finishing before the pot runs dry earns one. If the last cup goes before the post is done, Pip heads home to brew a fresh pot and starts the route again. Undo and restart are free.

A playable sketch of the proposal's rules: rotate, slide with wraparound, rooted tiles, tea per move, pie timers, the cat, signed letters and footbridges. Stars are saved in this browser only.
